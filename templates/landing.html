{# ================= FEATURES ================= #}

Everything a dictation session needs

Listen at your speed

Play each dictation at 60 to 120 words a minute. The same recording runs faster or slower without changing the voice.

Exam-style timing

Five minutes to read your notes, then 50 minutes on the typing pad. The clock keeps running if you refresh, and your text is submitted when it reaches zero.

Marked like the real test

Missing, added and wrong words are full mistakes. Spelling, singular and plural slips, capitals and full stops are half mistakes.

Weak words

Every word you mistype goes on your list, with the number of times it has tripped you up.

Stroke Techniques & Expert Joinings

After you submit, open the model strokes for that passage and compare them with your own notes.

Practice sets

Progressive, Kailashchandra and short dictation for beginners, each with your best score beside it.
